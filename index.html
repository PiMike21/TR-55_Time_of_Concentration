Calculation basis

Travel time. For velocity-based segments, Tt = L / (3600 V), where length is in feet, velocity is in feet per second, and Tt is in hours.

Sheet flow. The TR-55 kinematic sheet-flow relation is Tt = 0.007(nL)^0.8 / (P2^0.5 s^0.4). TR-55 limits this procedure to sheet-flow lengths of 300 ft or less.

Shallow concentrated flow. This app uses the Appendix F equations corresponding to Figure 3-1: V = 16.1345 sqrt(s) for unpaved flow and V = 20.3282 sqrt(s) for paved flow.

Open channel flow. Hydraulic radius is R = A/Pw. Velocity is computed with the US customary Manning equation: V = (1.49/n) R^(2/3) s^(1/2).

Time of concentration. Calculated Tc is the sum of travel times along the consecutive flow path. The app separately reports the TR-55 minimum Tc of 0.1 hr when the calculated value is smaller.

Engineering limitations

  • Field inspection and hydraulic-path selection remain fundamental; the calculator does not determine the hydraulically most distant path for you.
  • Channel calculations require representative cross-sectional flow area and wetted perimeter. TR-55 notes that channel velocity is usually determined for bankfull elevation.
  • Storm sewers, culverts with material storage, reservoirs, and pressure-flow systems may require hydraulic methods outside this simplified travel-time workflow.
  • Warnings identify common method limits but do not establish jurisdictional acceptability.

Reference basis: USDA-SCS Technical Release 55, Urban Hydrology for Small Watersheds, Chapter 3 and Appendix F.
